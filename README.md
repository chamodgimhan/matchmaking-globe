# Matchmaking Globe

An interactive globe that shows who should meet whom at a networking event. Every attendee is a name on a rotating globe. Click a person to see who they match with and why.



All 36 people and companies in this demo are made up.

## Why I built it

At big networking events most people end up talking to whoever is standing next to them. I wanted to see if a simple visual could help attendees find the few people actually worth meeting, before the event starts. This came out of my thesis research on matchmaking at professional events.

## What it does

- Shows every attendee as a name on a globe you can spin and zoom
- Click a name to open their profile and their suggested matches
- Upload your own attendee list as a CSV to try it with different data

## How it's built

One HTML file, no build step and no server. Open `index.html` in a browser and it works. The globe is drawn on a canvas, so it runs on normal laptops and phones.

## Run it locally

Download `matchmap-offline.html` and open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
